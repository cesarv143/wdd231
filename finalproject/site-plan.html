Aura & Essence

Website Plan Document

1. Site Name

Proposed Name: Aura & Essence

Reason for Selection: "Aura" represents the invisible sensory projection and presence a fragrance creates around a person, while "Essence" refers to the core olfactive notes and artisanal oils used in perfumery. This name communicates luxury, sophistication, and discovery.

Optional domain availability: aura-essence-perfumes.org

2. Site Purpose

The primary purpose of Aura & Essence is to simplify online fragrance discovery for enthusiasts and casual shoppers. Because scent cannot be smelled over the internet, the platform provides an intuitive, visual, and data-driven directory where users can explore fragrance families, dissect top/heart/base notes, filter scents based on longevity and occasion, and save their favorite selections to client-side storage. It also offers a personalized recommendation service via an interactive consultation form.

3. Target Audience Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: "I am looking for a long-lasting, woody fragrance for formal evening events, but I don't know which scent notes (like cedar or amber) fit that profile."
  • Scenario 2: "How can I request a customized fragrance recommendation based on scents I already like?"

4. Color Scheme

The site plan and final web application use an elegant, dark-mode luxury color scheme designed for high contrast and readability:

Primary: Charcoal (#121212) Main background color
Secondary: Warm Dark (#1A1A1A) Card backgrounds and structural blocks
Accent: Gold / Amber (#C5A059) Headings, active links, buttons, and highlights
Text: Off-White (#F4F4F4) Body text and paragraph content

5. Typography

To establish a balance between classic luxury branding and high technical legibility, two Google Fonts are implemented:

Heading Font: Cinzel (Serif)

Used for primary site titles, major section headings (H1, H2, H3), and brand elements.

Body Font: Montserrat (Sans-Serif)

Used for main body paragraphs, navigational links, form inputs, button labels, and fragrance card metadata.
